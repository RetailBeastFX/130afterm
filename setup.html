Forex Engine

Coinexx + MT5
STEP 01

Create Your Coinexx Account

Coinexx is an unregulated broker that I personally use and trust. It allows for high leverage and crypto funding, bypassing PDT rules and heavy restrictions.

Create Account
STEP 02

Fund via Crypto

Once registered, navigate to the funding section. I recommend using Litecoin (LTC) or XRP for the lowest network fees and fastest transfer times from your crypto exchange (like Coinbase or Kraken) to Coinexx.

STEP 03

Connect MetaTrader 5

Download MetaTrader 5. Coinexx will email you your server details (e.g., Coinexx-Live), account number, and master password. Enter these into MT5 to link your funded account to the trading terminal.

Download MT5 Windows | Download MT5 Mobile

Options Engine

Webull
STEP 01

Open a Webull Cash Account

For options, I prefer a Webull Cash Account over a Margin Account. A cash account completely avoids the Pattern Day Trader (PDT) rule, meaning you can day trade options with your settled cash every single day without needing $25k. Options cash settles overnight.

Create Webull Account
STEP 02

Apply for Options Permissions

Inside the app, navigate to your account settings and apply for Options Trading (Level 2). This allows you to buy calls and puts. You will need to fill out a brief experience questionnaire to get approved by Webull.

STEP 03

Configure Active Trader

Download Webull Desktop. In the Active Trader widget, set default order quantities to fixed contract sizes and configure 1-Click Instant Order Placement. Map hotkeys for immediate Market/Limit offsets so you can enter SPY 0DTE momentum breakouts and cut losing trades instantly.
