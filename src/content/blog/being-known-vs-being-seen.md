There's a particular kind of loneliness that comes from being around people who know of you but don't really know you.

They know your role. They know what you do. They know what you look like when you walk into the room. They might know basic facts about your life.

But they don't know what's going on inside you.

They don't see the thoughts you keep to yourself, the things you notice but don't say, or how much you're actually thinking about everything around you.

You're physically present, but emotionally invisible.

They know your role, but they don't know you.

Being treated like a body that occupies a space in somebody's life isn't the same thing as being known.

And there's a huge difference between the two.
