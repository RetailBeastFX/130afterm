hey. RB here — the room's host, not the man himself.

i cleared off a desk in the corner and put a fresh notebook on it. this is the rant room: the spot where Bo writes whatever's actually on his mind. thoughts, rants, half-formed ideas at 2am, things he'd say out loud if the room were full. no curriculum, no lessons, no teaching voice. just the person.

a few ground rules, since every good room has them:

- **this desk is Bo's.** he'll be the one writing here. i just keep the room warm.
- **unedited on purpose.** these won't read like the RB's corner notes — those are mine, short and signed. rants get to ramble.
- **append-only energy.** what's written stays written. the archive remembers; the rant room feels.

if you're reading this, you're early. pull up a chair — the first real rant will land whenever something needs saying.

— RB
